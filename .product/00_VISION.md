# Product Vision

## Mission

Empower Amol and Shailja (and optionally a small group of friends) to trade Indian equities algorithmically — with discipline, risk control, and continuous learning — without needing to watch the screen.

## Users

| Profile | Capital | Risk Level | Mode | Goal |
|---------|---------|-----------|------|------|
| amol | ₹1,00,000 | AGGRESSIVE | Paper Trading | Build + test strategies, eventually go live |
| shailja | ₹1,00,000 | (configurable) | Paper Trading | Learn systematic trading, avoid emotion |

## Core Principles

1. **Paper first** — Never execute real money until a strategy proves itself in paper mode for weeks
2. **Alerts not attention** — The app should notify via Telegram so users don't need to watch the screen
3. **Discipline over discretion** — Strategy rules decide trades, not gut feeling
4. **Learn from every trade** — Journal, stats, and performance tracking built in
5. **VIX-aware** — Risk parameters auto-adjust with India VIX to protect capital in volatile markets

## North Star

**Consistent positive P&L in paper trading over 30+ trades per profile before switching to live.**

## Roadmap

```
Phase 1: Core Platform ✅
  Real-time data, indicators, strategy engine, order management

Phase 2: Personal Use Features ✅
  Profiles, paper trading, Telegram alerts, VIX integration, strategy library

Phase 3: Advanced Scanning ✅
  Order Radar, Stock Scanner, Sector Heatmap, Alert Manager

Phase 4: Backtesting (NEXT)
  Run strategies against historical data before deploying live

Phase 5: Go Live
  Real money execution with tight risk limits
  Confidence gates: win rate > 55%, Sharpe > 1.0 in paper for 60+ trades

Phase 6: Group Features (Optional)
  Shared watchlists, leaderboard, Discord integration
```

## The Learning Flywheel

```
Activate strategy (paper mode)
        ↓
Telegram alerts notify of every trade
        ↓
Review trade journal daily
        ↓
Understand what worked, what didn't
        ↓
Tune strategy or swap it out
        ↓
Win rate and Sharpe improve
        ↓
Confidence to go live
```
